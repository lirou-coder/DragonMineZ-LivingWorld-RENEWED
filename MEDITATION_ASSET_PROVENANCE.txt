Integrated Meditation — Custom Particle Asset Provenance
=========================================================

The following original addon particle textures were generated specifically for the
Meditation project with OpenAI image generation and then manually/post-process edited
for use as small transparent Minecraft particle sprites:

  src/main/resources/assets/dbzmeditation/textures/particle/anime_ki_mote.png
  src/main/resources/assets/dbzmeditation/textures/particle/anime_ki_wisp.png
  src/main/resources/assets/dbzmeditation/textures/particle/anime_ground_flare.png
  src/main/resources/assets/dbzmeditation/textures/particle/anime_meditation_glyph.png

Design direction
- Original grayscale anime-style energy shapes.
- No requested text, logos, real alphabet/kanji, copyrighted character, or copied
  game/anime emblem.
- Transparent RGBA output sized for Minecraft particle rendering.

Post-processing
- Background/chroma remnants were removed.
- Assets were converted to neutral grayscale while preserving alpha and resized to
  64x64 RGBA.
- Transparent corners, non-empty visible bounds, neutral RGB channels and alpha were
  checked before integration.

These assets are shipped only under the dbzmeditation namespace. The release package
does not intentionally redistribute Dragon Mine Z textures/models or copied anime/game
artwork as Meditation assets.
