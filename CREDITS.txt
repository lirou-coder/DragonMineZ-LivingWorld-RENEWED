Dragon Mine Z: Living World — Credits
=====================================

DragonMineZ
  Required gameplay foundation. Living World deliberately reuses Dragon Mine Z's native combat, movement, forms, Ki techniques, items and fighter systems instead of replacing them.

Minecraft Forge
  Mod loader and development environment.

GeckoLib
  Fighter model/animation rendering support.

Integrated Meditation
  The former Dragon Mine Z Meditation 4.4.0 subsystem is now maintained directly inside Living World. It provides depth stages, TP progression, rare breakthroughs, form-mastery practice, shared NPC meditation and original meditation visual assets. Image Training/Image Spar is retired.

Integrated Living World Fusion
  Player/NPC and NPC/NPC fusion code from the final Living World Fusion Bridge 0.2.4 line is now maintained as part of Living World itself.
