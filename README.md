# DragonMine Z: Living World

**Turn Dragon Mine Z into a world that remembers.**

Living World adds persistent fighters, evolving relationships, factions, rivalries, equipment, goals, and history around Dragon Mine Z's existing combat and progression systems.

## 🧑 Persistent Fighters

Fighters can become recurring characters with their own power, personality, combat style, equipment, techniques, goals, relationships and battle history.

## ⚔️ Native DMZ Combat & Progression

Living World fighters use Dragon Mine Z's real combat systems: melee, Ki attacks, flight, transformations, racial progression, training and native equipment.

## 🤝 Relationships & Rivalries

Repeated encounters can develop into friendships, rivalries, mentorships, students and companions. Rivals can continue growing after surviving battles with you.

## 🗡️ Equipment & Inheritance

Fighters can own, use, recover and inherit real Dragon Mine Z equipment, including armor, Blaster Cannons and swords. Important weapons can pass between different owners throughout your world.

## 🥋 Technique Lineage

Fighters can learn real Dragon Mine Z techniques from mentors and other warriors, with the mod remembering who taught whom.

## 🎯 Individual Goals

Fighters pursue practical goals such as learning a technique, defeating a rival, obtaining equipment, learning flight or beating stronger opponents.

## 📖 Living History

Major victories, rival battles, techniques, mentorship, equipment inheritance, fusion, progression and deaths become part of each fighter's persistent story.

## 🌍 Dynamic World Activity

Encounter wandering warriors, factions, patrols, training groups, wanted fighters, conflicts and Guardian Corps responses while exploring.

## 🔍 Fighter Profiles

Shift + Right-click a Living World fighter to view their relationships, goal, equipment, techniques and history. Shift + Right-click with a Dragon Mine Z Scouter for additional combat information.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.20.1
- Forge 47.4.x
- Dragon Mine Z 2.1.3
- GeckoLib 4.8.4+
